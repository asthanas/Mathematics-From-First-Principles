# Study Plan

## Daily 90 Minutes
10 min recall → 25 min learning → 15 min examples → 25 min exercises → 10 min implementation/visualization → 5 min journal.

## Weekly
Monday: Learn
Tuesday: Practice
Wednesday: Derive
Thursday: Implement
Friday: Apply
Saturday: Review
Sunday: Reflect/rest

## Spaced Review
Same day → 1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 1 month.

## Mastery Gate
Aim for 80–90% accuracy on foundational exercises, plus the ability to solve unfamiliar problems and explain the reasoning.
